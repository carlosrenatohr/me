# Product Vision

## Product
Carlos Renato — Personal Portfolio V1

## Vision
Create a personal digital space for an independent software engineer and product builder. The portfolio should communicate professional judgment, product thinking, technical ownership, and the ability to turn real problems into useful software.

## Primary audience
- Technical recruiters
- Engineering managers and CTOs
- Founders and product teams
- Potential collaborators and clients
- Software engineering peers

## Primary visitor questions
1. Who is Carlos?
2. What kinds of problems and products does he work on?
3. What has he actually built?
4. What was his role and technical contribution?
5. How can someone contact him or review his CV?

## Positioning
Independent Software Engineer / Builder.

Suggested message direction:
"Building products. Solving problems. Making technology useful."

This is a draft positioning statement, not mandatory final copy.

## Product principles
- Projects are the primary proof of experience.
- Explain context, contribution, technical decisions, and verified outcomes.
- Show substance rather than a long list of technologies.
- Be personal without becoming informal or gimmicky.
- Prefer clarity and credibility over visual effects.
- Never publish confidential or unverified information.

## V1 outcome
A polished, responsive public portfolio that can be shared with recruiters, engineering leaders, founders, and collaborators.

## Explicitly out of scope
CMS, admin dashboard, authentication, database, backend API, user management, analytics dashboard, and complex content editing.
