# Visual Exploration

## Required concepts

### Direction A — Dark Editorial
Explore expressive typography, editorial pacing, large project imagery, asymmetric composition, restrained navy surfaces, and carefully placed blue accents.

### Direction B — Dark Technical Editorial
Explore a more structured editorial grid, technical metadata, numbered project sections, subtle rules, diagrams, and a stronger engineering-documentation character.

These are starting hypotheses. The designer may propose better names and refinements, but both directions must remain dark and navy-led.

## Each direction must show
- Home desktop and mobile.
- Project Detail desktop and mobile.
- Color palette with accessible text pairings.
- Typography pairing and scale.
- Grid and spacing.
- Image/art direction.
- Navigation behavior.
- Motion principles.
- Rationale and tradeoffs.

## Evaluation criteria
1. Does it feel personal and distinctive?
2. Does it communicate independent product engineering?
3. Are projects clearly the primary proof of experience?
4. Is the dark navy identity strong but restrained?
5. Is typography readable and memorable?
6. Does it avoid generic AI portfolio patterns?
7. Does it work at mobile sizes?

## Approval gate
Do not implement all pages until the owner selects or combines a direction.
