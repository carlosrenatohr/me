# Personal Design Profile — Carlos Renato

Status: Owner-approved preferences
Scope: Personal portfolio and future personal digital products

## Core preference
The owner strongly prefers entirely dark interfaces. Dark mode is mandatory for this portfolio. The favorite color is dark blue/deep navy, with Nativerse serving as an important visual reference.

## Desired personality
- Independent
- Thoughtful
- Technical
- Modern
- Refined
- Practical
- Confident without arrogance

## Visual direction
Dark Editorial: a blend of editorial composition and understated technical character.

The site should feel like a carefully designed personal digital space, not a corporate agency site, generic developer template, or SaaS dashboard.

## Non-negotiable constraints
- No light-mode design.
- No white, cream, ivory, beige, or warm-paper page backgrounds.
- No sage-green editorial palette as the primary direction.
- Deep navy/dark blue must be central to the identity.
- Avoid bright purple gradients, excessive neon, and oversaturated blue.
- Do not copy Nativerse's logo or create the impression this is a Nativerse corporate site.
- Do not inherit Orbit or Hit Cargo branding.

## Initial palette candidates
These are starting points to explore, not final tokens:
- Deep Navy: `#080D18`
- Navy: `#101A2B`
- Slate Blue: `#17253B`
- Steel Blue: `#315B91`
- Soft Blue: `#6698D1`
- Soft White: `#E5EAF2`

The designer must verify text contrast and accessibility. Introduce neutral cool grays where needed for depth and hierarchy.

## Typography
Explore a distinctive editorial serif for selected headlines, a highly readable sans-serif for body/interface, and a restrained monospace for technical metadata. Do not select fonts solely because they are fashionable.

## Composition
- Strong editorial hierarchy.
- Generous negative space.
- Intentional alignment and grid.
- Large, well-composed project imagery.
- Restrained borders and shadows.
- Avoid forcing every section into rounded cards.
- Asymmetry is welcome when it improves storytelling.

## Motion
Use subtle, purposeful transitions. Avoid gratuitous parallax, animated backgrounds, cursor tricks, or motion that harms readability, accessibility, or performance. Respect reduced-motion settings.

## Photography and imagery
A professional portrait may be used subtly and must remain optional. Prefer authentic project screenshots, diagrams, architecture illustrations, and approved photography. Never present generic imagery as an actual product screenshot.

## Nativerse reference
If the owner supplies Nativerse assets or screenshots, inspect them for color relationships, typography, proportions, and visual personality. Use them as inspiration only. Do not assume access to brand files that have not been provided.

## Approval
Before full implementation, present at least two genuinely distinct dark editorial directions, each with desktop and mobile concepts for Home and Project Detail. Explain the rationale and request owner approval.
